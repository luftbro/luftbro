## Luftbro 1.0

Luftbro plays your own video files, MKV included, on an Apple TV, with the Mac as the remote.

- MKV and other files the Apple TV can't open play as a stream made while you watch. Video and audio are copied as they are, so an MKV, MP4 or MOV starts in about a second. Downloads still in progress play too.
- Audio and subtitle tracks switch while playing. Text subtitles and Blu-ray picture subtitles (read as text by macOS) both work. The subtitle delay is one pick, or H / J in half seconds. Each file keeps its delay, and each series remembers your audio, subtitles and delay.
- Subtitles → Delay → Sync Automatically listens for the speech and moves the subtitles to match. Subtitles made for another frame rate are stretched to fit from start to finish.
- Skip Intro and Next Episode, found by their sound, and in the picture when the file has no chapters. Space presses the button while it's new, ⏭ any time, and the timeline marks where they are.
- The poster is the file's cover art, or the title card read from the opening titles.
- The timeline is the film drawn in its own colours, with a frame preview on hover. The window takes its background and accent colour from the film.
- Diagnostics slides out beside the window with the likely cause of a stutter (network, encoder, Apple TV, source file), live charts, every segment request and an event log. It records all the time, so you can open it afterwards.
- This Mac plays in a window on the Mac, with brightness, contrast, saturation, Fit / Fill and volume, and keeps the screen on while it plays.
- While a film plays on the TV, the Mac's screen goes dark. Space, the arrows and the media keys still work in the dark, and the mouse brings the screen back. A switch beside the sleep timer turns it off.
- The remote is one narrow column with the library underneath, which you can browse while something plays. TV volume, media keys, Control Center, a sleep timer, a Touch Bar in the film's colour, and every key in Help → Keyboard Shortcuts (⌘/).
- Your place is kept, also when you stop with the Apple TV's remote. A film counts as watched from its last few minutes, and "Stopped at 1:02:03 · 2 h ago" says when you last watched.
- A file that goes away mid-film (an unplugged disk, a dropped network share) ends playback with the reason.
- The colour timeline, intro and credits, poster and subtitle work stay with a file when its download finishes or it is renamed or moved.
- Luftbro checks on the backend, the Apple TVs and playback only as often as needed, and keeps quiet during a long pause.
- Updates install only when signed with a key that isn't kept on GitHub, and the download carries a GitHub build attestation (`gh attestation verify`). A failed update check says why in a sentence, with the details on request.
- The backend answers only to Luftbro, each stream has a secret address, and your watch history and Apple TV pairing are readable only by your account.
- Luftbro ejects its disk image when you first open it. ffmpeg 8.1.3 and Python 3.14.10 are inside.
